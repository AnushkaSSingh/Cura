# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where is my order?

Cura can check the customer's order status and expected delivery information.

## My order is delayed. What should I do?

Cura checks the order status and identifies whether the delivery is delayed.

## I was charged twice. What should I do?

Cura checks the payment records for duplicate charges. If a duplicate charge is verified, the case can proceed according to the refund policy.

## Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the applicable support policy and verified payment information.

## Why am I being transferred to a human agent?

If Cura cannot safely verify or resolve an issue automatically, it escalates the case with the available investigation context.

## Do I need to explain my problem again?

Cura can use available conversation history and previous support information so that the human agent receives the existing context.

## What happens when my case is escalated?

The human support agent receives the customer's original message, detected issue, investigation results, relevant records, escalation reason, and recommended next step.
